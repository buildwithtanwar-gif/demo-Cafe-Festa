OUR STORY

A little place with a lot of heart.

Tucked away in the busy streets of Paharganj, Cafe Festa serves fresh, comforting food and coffee in a warm atmosphere.

From relaxed breakfasts to satisfying lunches, dinners and coffee, our menu brings together Indian favourites, continental comfort food, Chinese dishes and café classics.
